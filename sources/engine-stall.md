# 2011 Chevrolet Equinox owner's manual, page 226, "If the engine stops while driving"

paraphrase: the page refuses automated fetches (HTTP 403), so these lines are as summarized by web search on Nov 6, 2026.

- paraphrase: If the engine stops while driving, brake normally but do not pump the brakes.
- paraphrase: The normal amount of power steering assist should return shortly after a few normal steering movements.

Second source, Consumer Reports, "What to do if your engine dies when you're driving", https://www.consumerreports.org/cro/news/2014/03/what-to-do-if-your-engine-dies-when-you-re-driving/index.htm (not fetched, paraphrase from search):
- paraphrase: If the engine quits while you are under way, the car loses power steering and soon power brakes. You can still guide and stop the car, but it takes longer and much more effort.
