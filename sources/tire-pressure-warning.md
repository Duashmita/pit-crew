NHTSA tire safety guidance: an underinflated tire runs hot and can fail; when the tire pressure warning light comes on, check the pressure in all tires as soon as it is safe and inflate to the pressure on the door placard, or fit the spare if a tire is flat. A flashing tire pressure light means the monitoring system itself has a fault.
